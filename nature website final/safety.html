Stay Safe in the Natural State

Essential tips for a safe and enjoyable outdoor experience.

🌲 Trail Safety

  • Stay on marked trails: Off-trail hiking can lead to getting lost or damaging local flora.
  • The "Buddy System": Never hike alone in unfamiliar territory.
  • Wildlife: Keep a safe distance from all wildlife and never feed them.

🎒 Packing Essentials

Always carry a map, compass, first-aid kit, and plenty of water.
