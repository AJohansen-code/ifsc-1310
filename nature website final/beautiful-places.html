10 breathtaking places of Arkansas

Explore our vast landscapes, scenic trails, and diverse wildlife.

1. Buffalo National River

America's First National River, the Buffalo is the crown jewel of Arkansas outdoors. Known for its towering limestone bluffs, it's a paradise for paddlers and hikers alike.

2. Petit Jean State Park

Near Morrilton, this little piece of heaven on earth features stunning vistas from Mather Lodge and a huge waterfall at the end of the Cedar Creek trail. Enjoy the rustic CCC architecture and historic trails.

3. Arkansas Grand Canyon

Located near Jasper along Scenic Byway 7, this "little grand canyon" is the ultimate roadside stop. You don't have to hike to see it, and it offers incredible views, especially during the fall foliage season.
