📍 Premier Fishing Spots

The White River

Famous worldwide for its Rainbow and Brown Trout. The tailwaters below Bull Shoals Dam are a must-visit for fly-fishing enthusiasts.

Lake Ouachita

Arkansas's largest lake, known for its crystal-clear water and abundance of Striped Bass, Crappie, and Bream.

The Arkansas River

A premier destination for Largemouth Bass tournaments, specifically around Lake Dardanelle and Pine Bluff.

🎣 Fishing Licenses

In Arkansas, anyone 16 or older is required to have a valid fishing license. Here is how you can get yours:

Online

Buy 24/7 at agfc.com. You can save a digital copy to your phone immediately.

In Person

Visit any AGFC regional office, nature center, or authorized local dealers like Walmart or bait shops.

Resident License: ~$10.50 | 3-Day Trip: ~$6.50

*Prices may vary. A separate Trout Permit is required for specific waters.
